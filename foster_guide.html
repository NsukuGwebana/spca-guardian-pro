Foster Guide

Follow these steps to successfully foster an animal and provide temporary care and love.

Step 1: Choose Your Animal

Browse our animals available for fostering and select one you can care for temporarily.

Step 2: Fill in the Foster Form

Complete the online foster application form with your personal details and availability.

Step 3: Application Review

Our staff will review your application and contact you if more information is required.

Step 4: Wait & Track

Check your foster application status on our website. If approved, we will schedule a pickup or home visit.

During Foster

Provide care, attention, and a safe environment for your foster pet while following SPCA guidelines.

Ongoing Support

Our team will provide guidance, tips, and check-ins to help ensure a smooth fostering experience.

Important Notes

Bring a copy of your ID and any other required documents for verification. Follow all SPCA guidance to ensure the well-being of the foster pet.
