Monthly Adoption Fair

Find your new best friend!

Event Details

  • Date: Every first Saturday of the month
  • Time: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM
  • Location: SPCA Guardian Pro Shelter, Old Bay Road, Makahanda, 6139

What to Expect

Come meet our wonderful animals in person! You can:

  • Interact with adoptable dogs, cats, and small animals.
  • Speak with our knowledgeable staff and volunteers.
  • Learn about our easy and compassionate adoption process.
  • Find out how you can volunteer or donate to help our cause.
