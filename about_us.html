About SPCA Guardian Pro

We are a passionate community dedicated to making a difference in the lives of animals.

Our Mission

Our goal at SPCA Guardian Pro is to give all animals, no matter how big or small, a safe place. We are dedicated to saving animals from dire circumstances, giving them thorough medical attention and rehabilitation, and helping them find devoted, long-term homes. Beyond rehoming, we also aim to promote animal rights and educate the public about ethical pet ownership. We strive relentlessly to ensure that every animal has the opportunity to live a happy and healthy life because we feel that every animal deserves that.

Our Story

A small group of animal enthusiasts founded SPCA Guardian Pro in February 2025 as a grassroots initiative to aid stray and abandoned animals in our neighborhood. With a group of committed employees and volunteers, what began as a tiny backyard has expanded into a fully furnished haven. By saving and rehoming hundreds of animals over time, we have built a network of kindhearted people who support our mission. We are appreciative of the ongoing assistance that enables us to increase our influence and reach, and we are proud of our journey.
